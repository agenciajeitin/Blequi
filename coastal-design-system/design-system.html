WebGL2 & Three.js Reference

Live Coastal Sky & Ocean Shader Controls

Ajuste os parâmetros em tempo real — o oceano WebGL2 rodando ao fundo responde instantaneamente a 60 FPS.

60 FPS Architecture Checklist (Coastal World Standard):
• Max 1 directionalLight with castShadow (shadow-mapSize: [1536, 1536])
• Use THREE.InstancedMesh for pines, palms, bushes, street lamps, pier planks, indoor plants
• Always include #include <colorspace_fragment> in custom GLSL ShaderMaterials
• Always call .dispose() on procedural geometries, shaders, and CanvasTextures on unmount
